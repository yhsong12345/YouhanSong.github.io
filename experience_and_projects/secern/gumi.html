Gumi MetaVerse (January 2024 ~ September 2024)

Client: Logistics Revolution Korea Co., Ltd.

Affiliation: SECERN AI

In this project, we developed an Object Detection Model to determine whether the worker is wearing PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) when they are passing the gate. The detection categories are safety helmet, safety vest, and box. (The detection box aims to prevent workers from taking company property outside) For selection of the model, we tested many different algorithms and chose the best-performing model.

The system achieved 95.42% accuracy.
