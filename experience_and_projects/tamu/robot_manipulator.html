1. Mechanics of Robotics (September 2021 ~ December 2021)

A robot arm with no more than 3 degrees of freedom was designed for factory applications such as classifying products, placing products in trays, and moving products. To achieve the desired performance, kinematics methods, control techniques, and adaptive control algorithms were used. With motivation from this project, robot arms with more than 3 degrees of freedom could be designed for different purposes in the future.

2. Dubin's Vehicle (Path Planning) (October 2021 ~ December 2021)

The Dubins vehicle (path planning) can be utilized in autonomous systems for robots. This helps to understand and calculate the optimal path between points. The Dubins vehicle algorithm was implemented in two different ways: CS and SC methods. In these methods, curves and segments are used to measure the optimal distance. The points and their angles are given as input. To calculate the length of a curve, the coordinates of a circle need to be computed. Through this project, similar algorithms could potentially be applied to other autonomous machines using more developed technology in the future.
