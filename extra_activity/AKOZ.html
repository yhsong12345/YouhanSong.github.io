AKOZ (August 2021 ~ August 2023)

AKOZ is a basketball club within the Korean Student Association at Texas A&M University. A group of Korean undergraduate and graduate students plays basketball once or twice a week during the semester. Besides playing basketball, we hang out at the beginning or end of the semester to build friendships. During the semester, we participate in intramural competitions to create meaningful memories during the academic year.
